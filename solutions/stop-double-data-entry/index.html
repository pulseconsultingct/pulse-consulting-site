Problem we solve

Stop typing the same information into two systems

A customer fills out a form, and someone copies it into a spreadsheet, then into accounting, then into the schedule. Every copy takes time and every copy is a chance for a typo. Connecting those systems is one of the most dependable ways to save staff time.

A short survey, no obligation. We review it before we talk.

How to tell you have this problem

  • Staff copy and paste between a website, a spreadsheet, and another program
  • Two reports about the same thing show different numbers
  • Someone's job includes 'entering orders' or 'updating the sheet'
  • Information from emails or PDFs gets retyped by hand
  • Nobody is sure which version of a customer's details is the right one

Ways to connect your systems

The right fix depends on the tools involved. In rough order of preference:

  1. A built-in connection that one of your tools already offers.
  2. A connector service that links the two tools with simple rules.
  3. A small custom script or integration, when the first two don't exist or don't fit.

We pick the simplest one that works, since that is also the one cheapest to keep running.

Where AI helps

When the information arrives messy, such as in an email, a scanned form, or a PDF invoice, AI can read it and pull out the fields you need. That removes the retyping step even when there is no clean connection between systems.

AI reads can be wrong. For anything involving money, dates, or legal terms, we build a quick review step so a person approves the result before it is saved.

What to watch for

  • Messy existing data. If your records have duplicates or inconsistent formats, we need to clean them up first or the connection will just copy the mess faster.
  • One source of truth. We decide with you which system is the master record, so edits don't overwrite each other.
  • Plan limits. Some tools only allow connections on higher plans, and some connectors charge by volume. We check this before building.
  • Breakage. If either tool changes how it works, the connection can stop. We aim to make failures visible.

Who benefits most

Any business that has at least two tools holding overlapping information: a website and a spreadsheet, booking and billing, an inbox and a customer list. The survey asks which tools you use, and that is usually enough to see whether a connection is possible.

Common questions

Do my tools have to be modern to be connected?

Not always. Older tools sometimes offer file exports or email notifications we can work with, and AI can read documents when there is no direct connection. We'll tell you if a connection isn't practical.

Is my data safe when systems are connected?

It depends on the tools and how they are set up. We explain where your information travels before we start. For legal or compliance questions, check with a qualified professional.

What if I'm not sure which of my tools can connect?

That's a normal starting point. List them in the survey and we'll check what is possible.
