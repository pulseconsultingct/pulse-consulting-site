Problem we solve

Automate appointment booking and reminders

If scheduling means phone calls, back-and-forth texts, and a calendar someone has to update by hand, this is one of the easiest tasks to take off your plate. It is also one of the most common places where customers get frustrated.

Tell us about your business

A short survey, no obligation. We review it before we talk.

Signs this is costing you

  • Someone spends part of each day answering calls or messages just to find a time
  • Customers forget appointments, and the slot goes unused
  • You have been double-booked because two calendars didn't match
  • Rescheduling requires a phone call from both sides
  • New customers have to fill out the same paperwork every visit

What can be automated

  • Online booking that only shows times you are actually free
  • Instant confirmation by email or text
  • Reminders a day or so before, with a link to confirm or reschedule
  • A notice to the next person on a waiting list when a slot opens up
  • An intake form sent ahead of time, with answers filed where your staff can find them

Check what you already pay for first

Many booking tools already include reminders and online scheduling, and the settings are just turned off. Before building anything, we look at what you have. If a setting solves the problem, that is the cheapest and most reliable fix, and we'll say so.

When your tools don't cover it, we connect them or add the missing step.

Things to be careful about

  • Text messages to customers have consent and opt-out rules. Confirm the requirements for your business with a qualified professional.
  • If you handle health or other sensitive information, how it is collected and stored matters. We do not give legal advice, and we will raise the question before building.
  • Time zones, holidays, and buffer time between appointments are where scheduling setups usually fail. We test these deliberately.
  • A reminder that fails to send is worse than no reminder, because nobody notices. We build in a way to see failures.

Where this works well

Salons and barbershops, clinics and practices, fitness and wellness studios, repair and service trades, tutoring and lessons, and any business where customers book a time slot. If that sounds like you, start with the survey and tell us which booking tool you use today.

Common questions

Do I have to switch booking software?

Not necessarily. We first check whether your current tool can do what you need. Switching is a last resort because it disrupts your customers.

Will reminders reduce no-shows?

They often help, but we can't promise a number. We can set up the reminders and you will be able to see the difference in your own bookings.

Can customers still call to book?

Yes. Automation adds a faster option for people who prefer it. Staff can still book by phone, and those bookings go into the same calendar.
