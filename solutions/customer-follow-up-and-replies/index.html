Problem we solve

Faster customer follow-up without losing the personal touch

Most small businesses lose some customers for a simple reason: they didn't hear back in time. A quote request sits in an inbox for two days, or a happy customer never gets asked for a review. These gaps are fixable without turning your business into a robot.

Tell us about your business

A short survey, no obligation. We review it before we talk.

Where follow-up usually breaks down

  • New inquiries arrive by email, web form, text, and social media, and nobody sees them all
  • Quotes go out and nobody checks back
  • Reviews are never requested, or only when someone remembers
  • Customers ask the same five questions over and over
  • Replies depend on whoever happens to be at their desk

What can be automated

  • An instant acknowledgement so the customer knows you got their message and when to expect an answer
  • Gathering inquiries from different places into one list with an owner assigned
  • A reminder to follow up when a quote or inquiry has gone quiet
  • A review request sent after a job is finished
  • Draft replies for common questions, which you read and send

Keep a person in charge

We build these so that software prepares and a person approves, at least at first. AI-drafted replies can be wrong, can sound off, or can promise something you can't deliver. Reviewing a draft takes much less time than writing one, and you keep control of what customers see.

If you want something to send messages on its own, such as a plain 'we got your request' note, we keep it limited to things that can't go wrong.

Be upfront with customers

If a chat assistant on your website answers customer questions, customers should know it isn't a person, and it should hand off to you when it can't help. We recommend limiting it to questions it can answer reliably, such as hours, services, and how booking works, and sending everything else to you.

Things to check before starting

  • Messaging rules. Automated texts and marketing emails come with consent and opt-out requirements. Confirm yours with a qualified professional.
  • Your voice. We write templates in your tone, and you approve them before anything goes live.
  • Failure visibility. A follow-up system that silently stops is worse than none, so we include a way to see that it is running.

Common questions

Will customers know they're getting automated messages?

Simple confirmations are normal and expected. Anything that looks like a personal reply is drafted for you and sent by you, unless you choose otherwise.

Can this work for a business with just a few staff?

Yes. It is often most useful there, because the person answering messages is also doing everything else.

Do you handle the review requests for me?

We can set up the request to go out automatically after a job. We can't and won't write fake reviews, and requests should go to real customers only.
