Workflow automation for small businesses

Workflow automation means connecting the software you already use so routine steps happen without someone remembering to do them. A form gets filled out, and the right things happen next.

Tell us about your business

A short survey, no obligation. We review it before we talk.

What it looks like in practice

  • A customer submits an inquiry. It is logged in your spreadsheet or customer list, and they get an acknowledgement right away.
  • A booking is confirmed. The customer gets a reminder before the appointment, and your calendar updates.
  • A job is marked complete. An invoice draft is prepared and a follow-up message is queued for your review.
  • Every Monday, a short report of last week's numbers lands in your inbox without anyone building it by hand.

Where AI fits and where it doesn't

A lot of useful automation needs no AI at all. It is simple rules: when this happens, do that. We use plain rules wherever they are enough, because they are cheaper and more predictable.

AI helps when the input is messy. Reading an email and pulling out a name and a date, sorting incoming messages by topic, or drafting a reply for you to approve are good examples. In those cases we keep a person in the loop for anything that affects money or customers.

How we approach a project

  1. Map the current process: who does what, in which tool, and where it stalls.
  2. Pick one workflow to start with. A small first win beats a big project that never finishes.
  3. Build it with the tools you already have where possible.
  4. Test it on real examples, including the odd ones.
  5. Write down how it works so you or your team can run it, and know what to check if it stops.

What can go wrong

Automations break. A connected account expires, a tool changes its settings, or a customer does something unexpected. Good automation is built so failures show up where you can see them, not silently. We aim to set up alerts and a short checklist for when something looks off.

Some tools limit what can be connected on lower plans. If the setup would need a more expensive plan, we will tell you before we build anything.

Is it worth it for a small business?

Often, when a task is repeated many times a week and follows the same steps. Rarely, when it happens a few times a month or needs judgment every time. An honest answer to that question is the first thing we work out with you.

Common questions

Will I need to buy new software?

Often not. We start with what you already use. If a tool you use cannot connect to the others, or an upgrade is required, we will say so up front and let you decide.

What happens if an automation stops working?

We build in alerts where we can, and leave you a short written guide for checking the usual causes, so you know what to look at first.

Can you automate something that involves customer information?

Yes, with care. We will talk through where the information goes and who can see it before we build. For legal or compliance questions, check with a qualified professional.
