AI strategy and audits for small businesses

Before you buy any software or sign up for any AI tool, it helps to know where your time actually goes. An audit gives you that picture, along with a short, ranked list of what is worth doing first and what isn't.

Tell us about your business

A short survey, no obligation. We review it before we talk.

What an audit is

An audit is a structured look at how your business runs day to day. We are not auditing your finances or your compliance. We are looking for repetitive work, hand-offs that get dropped, and places where staff re-type the same information.

Most small businesses already know a few things that feel wasteful. The audit finds the rest, and it also tells you which of those things are actually worth fixing.

What we look at

  • The tasks your team repeats every day or every week, and how long they really take
  • The tools you already pay for (email, calendar, booking, accounting, spreadsheets) and whether they connect to each other
  • Where customers wait on you: inquiries, quotes, scheduling, follow-up
  • Where mistakes happen, such as double bookings, missed messages, or numbers that don't match
  • What data you keep, where it lives, and who can see it

What you get at the end

A written plan in plain language. Each item says what the problem is, what we would change, what it would involve, and what it would take to keep running afterward. Items are ranked, so you can do the first one and stop if that's all that makes sense.

The plan also includes the things we would not automate. Some tasks are faster done by a person, and some are risky to hand to software. We will tell you which is which.

How it works

  1. You fill out the short survey about your business and your tools.
  2. We read it, then have an intro call to ask follow-up questions.
  3. We review how things run and write up the plan.
  4. We go through it with you. You decide what, if anything, to do next.

Who this is a good fit for

Owners who suspect they are losing time to busywork but are not sure where, and who want an honest opinion before spending money. It also suits businesses that tried a tool and abandoned it.

It is not a good fit if you already know exactly what you want built. In that case, the custom builds or workflow automation pages are the better starting point.

What to expect on cost and upkeep

Every recommendation in the plan comes with a note on what it would cost to set up, what it would cost to keep running, and what could break. Software subscriptions, connected accounts, and changes to the tools you use all create ongoing work, and you should know that before you commit.

Common questions

Do I need to be technical to do an AI audit?

No. The survey and the call are written for business owners. You describe how your business works, and we handle the technical translation.

Will the audit tell me to buy a lot of software?

Not by design. Often the first finding is that a tool you already pay for can do the job. We list costs for every recommendation so you can judge for yourself.

What if AI isn't the right answer for my business?

Then we will say so. A plan that says 'fix this process first' or 'a spreadsheet is enough here' is a legitimate result.
